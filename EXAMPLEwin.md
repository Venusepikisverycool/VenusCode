Windows commands:

venuscode encode "Message" C:\Users\Yourname\Desktop\filename.png

venuscode decode C:\Users\Yourname\Desktop\filename.png

Two examples:

venuscode encode "https://www.wikipedia.org" C:\Users\Yourname\Desktop\filename.png

venuscode decode C:\Users\Yourname\Desktop\filename.png
